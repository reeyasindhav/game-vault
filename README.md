Problem Statement:

Gamers often play across multiple consoles and platforms, making it difficult to keep track of their unplayed games, discover tailored new titles, and find reliable reviews in a single place. Without a unified system, players struggle to organize their gaming backlog, rate their experiences, and receive personalized recommendations tailored to their unique preferences. Gamevault solves this by offering a centralized discovery platform where gamers can seamlessly track, review, and explore titles across all gaming systems.
